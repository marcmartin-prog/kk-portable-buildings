Features and Options

Built on 4x6 Pressure Treated Skids

Creating the strongest subfloors in the industry with 16" centers on our floor joists!

Tongue and Groove

Built with tongue and groove flooring for award-winning performance you can rely on. Specifically engineered to industry-leading strength, superior moisture resistance and installation ease.

LP SmartSide Siding

SP SmartSide Siding allows you to combine style and advanced durability so that your building will maintain its integrity and beauty over time.

Floor Joists

We use the most 2x4s in the portable building industry for the strongest sub-floor system.

Studs and Rafters

Chateau and Classic Series have 16" on center even with a metal roof. Top quality grade lumber, rafter joints are double gussetted.

Sub-Roof Material

The roof is always built with sheeting no matter what style of roof you choose.

Roof

Name brand 35 year dimensional shingles, or 40 year metal roofing, all available in different colors of your choice.

Engineered Wood Trim

Top-performing engineered wood trim to get a look that lasts.

Windows w/ Screens and Shutters

Aluminum windows come standard in all storage buildings. Vinyl insulated windows come standard in all cabins. Many sizes and options available.

Delivery to Prepared Site

A level rock gravel pad is recommended (approximately 4 inches thick and slightly bigger than the buildings square footage). Ask us about getting custom piers set for a solid foundation for your building.

Features and Customization

Build a building just how you like it with our dozens of flexible custom building options. Whichever option you choose, it will be the most durable and quality building available.

Included on many is an operational locking loft door, double gussetted joints, and quality materials, features designed to improve and guarantee strength and durability.

And Much, Much More...

Features like a threshold protector are included at no extra charge. You can rely on our attention to details to get you a quality building that will last for years.
